Frequently Asked Questions

Clients arriving 15 minutes late will be required to pay a ₱500 late fee. Arriving 20 minutes late or more will result in forfeiture of the appointment and will be treated as a no-show.

We accept Cash, GCash, Bank Transfer (BDO, BPI), and Major Credit/Debit Cards. A non-refundable booking deposit is required upon reservation and will be deducted from your final bill.

Please get a good night's rest, eat a full meal before coming in, and stay well-hydrated. Avoid consuming alcohol or blood-thinning medications 24 hours prior to your session.

Our studio strictly enforces medical-grade sanitation protocols. We use 100% single-use, pre-sterilized disposable needles and autoclave equipment for all jewelry and tools.

Free street parking and customer slots are available directly in front of our studio building on a first-come, first-served basis.

You can book directly by clicking "BOOKING" in our top navigation bar, choosing your service and preferred date, and submitting your deposit receipt.

Rescheduling requests must be sent at least 48 hours before your appointment time. You may reschedule once without forfeiting your booking deposit.

If you cancel your appointment, booking deposits are strictly non-refundable. Cancellations made within 24 hours will require a new deposit to rebook.
