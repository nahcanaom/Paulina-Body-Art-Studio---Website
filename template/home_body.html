Studio Policies

  1. Booking an appointment is highly recommended to guarantee your preferred schedule. Limited walk-in slots may be available and are posted daily on our Instagram Stories. We encourage clients to message us first to confirm availability.
  2. A P500 reservation deposit is required to secure your appointment. This amount will be deducted from your total service cost during your visit. If any balance remains unused, it will be returned.
  3. Appointments without a deposit will not be confirmed. Please note that the deposit is non-refundable in case of cancellation or no-show.
  4. You may reschedule your appointment once, provided the request is made at least 48 hours before your scheduled time.
  5. Clients under 15 years of age must be accompanied by a parent or legal guardian, or present a signed consent letter along with valid IDs.
  1. Clients arriving 15 minutes late will be required to pay a P500 late fee. Arriving 20 minutes late or more will result in forfeiture of the appointment and will be treated as a no-show.
  1. Cashless payments are preferred.
  2. Accepted methods: GCash, GoTyme, Maya, or bank transfer.
  3. If paying in cash, please bring the exact amount.

Before your Appointment

  1. Eat a meal before your appointment.
  2. Avoid caffeine, alcohol, and pre-workout supplements beforehand.
  3. Do not take blood-thinning medications before your appointment.
  4. Come well-rested and hydrated.
  5. Avoid applying makeup or skincare products near the piercing area.
  6. Wear clothing that won't interfere with the piercing area.
  7. Companions are welcome but are not allowed inside the piercing room. Please observe silence during piercing sessions.
  1. For your safety, please postpone your appointment if you are pregnant, breastfeeding, feeling unwell, or recovering from any recent medical condition. Do not proceed if you have any active irritation, infection, or wounds near the piercing area, or if you have upcoming procedures or activities that may interfere with healing. Clients are required to disclose any relevant health information prior to booking to ensure a safe and appropriate service.
  1. Parking is available on-site, but it is on a first come, first served basis as managed by the building administration.
